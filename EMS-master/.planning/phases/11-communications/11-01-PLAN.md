From config/bms_layer2.dbc (10 message types, all little-endian Intel byte order):
- 0x00 PackSummary: pack_v(0|16 u 0.1), pack_i(16|16 s 0.1), pack_soc(32|8 u 0.5), pack_soh(40|8 u 0.5), fault_code(48|16 u 1)
- 0x01 CellVoltage_01: cell_v_01..cell_v_04 (each 16-bit unsigned, scale 0.001)
- 0x02-0x07 CellVoltage_02..07: same pattern, cells 5-28
- 0x08 CellTemperature: cell_t_01..cell_t_08 (each 8-bit signed, scale 1, offset -40)
- 0x09 RackStatus: max_cell_v, min_cell_v, avg_cell_v, max_cell_t, min_cell_t, avg_cell_t (various)
